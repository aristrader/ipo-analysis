# Predictor improvement ideas — THINK + PROPOSE (no code)

> Scope: make scoring a **NEW IPO** more useful/trustworthy for a real user. Post-build review of the
> shipped engine (`analogs.py` → `scorecard.py` → `predict.py`/`weights.py`). NO-ML, analog/comparables,
> transparent, honest-by-default — every proposal is checked against that and against the ACTUAL substrate
> columns + the validated rules in `rules/index.md`. Already-built (not re-proposed): hard-gate/Gower/widening
> ladder; the 5 components; presets + data-informed weights; the confidence checklist; named comparables +
> outcome distribution; min-N honesty. Ruthless: gimmicks are marked SKIP.

## Top BUILD picks (lead)

1. **Wire the VALIDATED signals into the components** (accrual flag + debt-death as explicit quality/downside sub-terms).
2. **Show the analog outcome DISTRIBUTION (histogram/sparkline), not just median + P10/P90.**
3. **Conflicting-analogs / bimodality detector** — flag a split cohort instead of reporting a misleading median.
4. **Liquidity-realizability haircut on downside-safety** (an illiquid −60% you can't exit).
5. **Calibration / reliability readout** ("when the score said top-quintile, what actually happened?") — re-display of an already-VALIDATED backtest.

## Final recommendation (top BUILD picks)

The highest-leverage, best-grounded additions all reuse work we already trust. **First, wire the two validated, hard-to-fake signals into the scorecard** — the accrual red-flag (positive PAT but negative operating cash) and the extreme-debt death cliff are among our only robust findings, yet the shipped `quality()`/`downside_safety()` ignore them; surface them as named flags, not buried numbers (#1). **Second, show the analog outcome distribution as a small histogram** alongside the median/P10/P90 (#2) and **flag bimodal/conflicting cohorts** so a barbell of multibaggers-and-wipeouts can't masquerade as a benign median (#3) — these two together fix the single biggest interpretive trap of analog-averaging, cheaply. **Third, add the liquidity-realizability haircut to downside-safety** (#4) so an unexitable SME loss isn't scored like a liquid one. **Finally, plumb the already-VALIDATED `strat-combined-score` backtest into a calibration readout** (#5): tell the user "top-quintile-score IPOs historically beat the field by +38pp, cross-regime; this one scores in the Nth quintile" — the cheapest, most credibility-building change available because it's a re-display, not new modelling. Bundle the small honesty wins with these: confidence-gated verdict (#11), most-similar-precedent callout (#6), and cohort-vs-base-rate line (#7). Hold recency *weighting*, manual gate-editing, and full what-if grids as MAYBE (disclose, don't auto-tune); skip GMP-in-score, fine-grained calibration curves, and multi-metric ensembles as scope-creep or gimmicks.
