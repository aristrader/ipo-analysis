F · The flip trap (hot IPO = big pop, tiny allotment)

The most popular IPO myth: apply to the heavily oversubscribed issue and flip the pop. The catch is arithmetic — the more oversubscribed an IPO, the bigger its pop BUT the smaller your allotment, and the two cancel. What a flipper captures per rupee applied is flat-to-declining across demand buckets, often worst for the hottest names. The pop is real; it's just not investable.
